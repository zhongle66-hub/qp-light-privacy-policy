Privacy Policy for QPLight

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to QPLight ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We do not collect any personal information that you voluntarily provide through the App. The App does not require user registration or account creation.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

The App may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration)
  • Crash reports and performance data (if enabled)

2.3 Bluetooth and Location Data

To provide core functionality, the App requires access to:

  • Bluetooth: To discover, connect to, and control BLE-enabled lighting devices
  • Location Services: Required by Android OS to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices
Important Notes:
  • We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your location data to any server
  • Location permission is only used locally on your device for Bluetooth scanning
  • Bluetooth connection data remains on your device and is not shared with third parties
  • Device names and MAC addresses are stored locally for your convenience

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To enable Bluetooth connectivity with lighting devices
  • To provide light control functionality (brightness, color, temperature)
  • To save your device pairing preferences locally
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To troubleshoot technical issues

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All data collected by the App is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Paired device information (device name, MAC address)
  • User preferences (brightness levels, color settings)
  • Application settings

4.2 No Server Communication

The App does NOT:

  • Upload any data to external servers
  • Share data with third-party services
  • Use cloud storage or synchronization
  • Track user behavior for advertising purposes

4.3 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your local data. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. All operations are performed locally on your device.

If we add third-party services in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View all data stored locally on your device
  • Delete: Remove paired devices and preferences through the App settings
  • Revoke Permissions: Disable Bluetooth or Location permissions through device settings
  • Uninstall: Completely remove the App and all associated data by uninstalling

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

1. 引言

欢迎使用潜派创新（QPLight）（以下简称"我们"）。我们致力于保护您的隐私并确保您的个人信息安全。本隐私政策说明了当您使用我们的移动应用程序（以下简称"应用"）时，我们如何收集、使用、披露和保护您的信息。

使用本应用即表示您同意按照本政策收集和使用信息。如果您不同意本隐私政策的条款，请勿访问本应用。

2. 我们收集的信息

2.1 您提供的信息

我们不收集您通过应用自愿提供的任何个人信息。应用不需要用户注册或创建账户。

2.2 自动收集的信息

应用可能会自动收集某些信息，包括：

  • 设备信息（设备型号、操作系统版本）
  • 应用使用数据（使用的功能、会话时长）
  • 崩溃报告和性能数据（如果启用）

2.3 蓝牙和位置数据

为提供核心功能，应用需要访问：

  • 蓝牙：用于发现、连接和控制支持 BLE 的照明设备
  • 位置服务：Android 系统要求扫描附近蓝牙设备时需要此权限
重要说明：
  • 我们不会收集、存储或向任何服务器传输您的位置数据
  • 位置权限仅在您的设备上本地用于蓝牙扫描
  • 蓝牙连接数据保留在您的设备上，不与第三方共享
  • 设备名称和 MAC 地址仅本地存储以便您使用

3. 我们如何使用您的信息

我们仅将收集的信息用于以下目的：

  • 实现与照明设备的蓝牙连接
  • 提供灯光控制功能（亮度、颜色、色温）
  • 本地保存您的设备配对偏好
  • 改善应用性能和用户体验
  • 排查技术问题

4. 数据存储和安全

4.1 本地存储

应用收集的所有数据都存储在您的设备本地，包括：

  • 配对设备信息（设备名称、MAC 地址）
  • 用户偏好（亮度级别、颜色设置）
  • 应用程序设置

4.2 无服务器通信

应用不会：

  • 向外部服务器上传任何数据
  • 与第三方服务共享数据
  • 使用云存储或同步功能
  • 出于广告目的跟踪用户行为

4.3 数据安全

我们实施适当的技术措施来保护您的本地数据。但请注意，没有任何通过互联网传输或电子存储的方法是 100% 安全的。

5. 第三方服务

应用未集成任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪服务。所有操作都在您的设备上本地执行。

如果我们在未来添加第三方服务，我们将相应更新本隐私政策。
